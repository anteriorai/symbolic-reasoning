# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Expected behavior:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people who use it

Unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission
- Posting real case data, such as patient records or personal information, in issues, pull requests or discussions; use synthetic cases instead
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

Report security vulnerabilities privately, as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and when someone officially represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to tech@anterior.com. Every report is reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and will explain moderation decisions when appropriate. Depending on the violation, they respond with one of:

1. **Correction:** a private, written warning that explains the violation.
2. **Warning:** a warning with consequences for continued behavior, and no interaction with the people involved for a set period.
3. **Temporary ban:** no interaction with the community for a set period, for a serious or sustained violation.
4. **Permanent ban:** for a pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of groups of people.
